Working with agents agentom My harness for coding with agents.
  1. 01 · The calls The agent builds, the decisions come back to me agentom is my harness for coding with agents. Even when I take the agent’s recommendation, I see every decision laid out with its options and context, as if we made it together. See it drawn in the tour →
